I. Institutional Philosophy, Vision, and Mission

Philosophy: The University of Perpetual Help System DALTA believes and invokes Divine Guidance in the betterment of the quality of life through national development and transformation, which are predicated upon the quality of education of its people. Towards this end, the Institution is committed to the ideals of teaching, community service and research as it nurtures the value of "Helpers of God", with "Character Building is Nation Building" as its guiding principle.

Vision: The University of Perpetual Help System DALTA shall emerge as a premier university in the Philippines. It shall provide a venue for the pursuit of excellence in academics, technology and research through local and international linkages. It shall produce Perpetualites as "Helpers of God", proud of their race and prepared for exemplary global participation.

Mission: The University of Perpetual Help System DALTA is dedicated to the development of the Filipino as a LEADER. It aims to graduate DYNAMIC STUDENTS who are physically, intellectually, socially and spiritually committed to the achievement of the highest quality of life. It is dedicated to the FORMATION OF CHRIST-CENTERED, SERVICE-ORIENTED, AND RESEARCH-DRIVEN INDIVIDUALS with great social concern and commitment to quality education.

Mission Keywords: A - Leader; B - Dynamic Students; C - Christ-centered, Service-Oriented & Research-Driven; D - Social Concern; E - Quality Education; F - Helpers of God.

Institutional Grading Policy

Base 0 Computation: Final marks are computed using standard criterion-referenced raw score percentage distribution.

Class Standing (70%): Includes hands-on laboratory exercises, programming practicals, quizzes, and project milestones.

Major Examinations (30%): Prelim Exam, Midterm Exam, and Final Exam / Capstone Defense.

Passing Grade: The minimum passing grade is 75.0% (Equivalent to 3.0).
